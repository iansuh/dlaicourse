## Method

- **Loudness**: ITU-R BS.1770-4 K-weighting (libebur128 filter constants, cross-checked against ffmpeg `ebur128`). *M-max* = highest loudness of any 400 ms window (sample hop, sound zero-padded so a short sound is measured whole); *S-max* = the same with a 3 s window; *I* = gated integrated loudness (files under 400 ms padded to one block). Effects are normalised and judged on **M-max**: for sounds of 30 ms to 2.5 s a 3 s short-term window mostly measures silence, so the 400 ms window is the meaningful 'short-term' figure for one-shots. Music is judged on I.
- **True peak**: 4x oversampled (polyphase), dBTP. **Clipping**: samples at |x| >= 0.999. **DC**: |mean| as a fraction of full scale.
- **Duration**: *audible ms* runs to the last sample above -60 dBFS and is checked against the cue budget; *file ms* includes the 20 ms of digital silence appended to very short effects (keeps codec noise off the end).
- **Lead silence**: time to the first sample above -50 dBFS (rule for effects: < 5 ms). **End**: peak of the last 2 ms (rule: <= -50 dBFS, so nothing is cut off with a click).
- **Phone loss**: how much loudness disappears through a 2nd-order 300 Hz high-pass, a rough model of a phone speaker. Above 6 LU means the cue relies on bass a phone cannot play.
- **Loop seam** (music): a click is a short burst of high frequencies. The file is looped (end joined to sample 0) and high-passed at 6 kHz (4th order, with circular context so the filter is settled). The seam passes if the peak within 2 ms of the join is no higher than 2x the loop's own 99th percentile of 2 ms block peaks (the join is no rougher than the music itself) **or** lies below -60 dBFS (at least 34 dB under the music's loudness, masked). Also: loudness of the last vs. the first 400 ms within 3 LU, and, for information, the second-difference ratio (|x[n+1] - 2x[n] + x[n-1]| at the join vs. its 99th percentile). Self-test: the same loop cut at 55 % and rejoined at 80 % must fail. The seam is checked on the decoded OGG because the encoder codes the last and first blocks independently; `generate.py` raises the Vorbis quality until it passes.
