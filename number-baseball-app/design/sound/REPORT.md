## Method

- **Loudness**: ITU-R BS.1770-4 K-weighting (libebur128 filter constants, cross-checked against ffmpeg `ebur128`). *M-max* = highest loudness of any 400 ms window (sample hop, sound zero-padded so a short sound is measured whole); *S-max* = the same with a 3 s window; *I* = gated integrated loudness (files under 400 ms padded to one block). Effects are normalised and judged on **M-max**: for sounds of 30 ms to 2.5 s a 3 s short-term window mostly measures silence, so the 400 ms window is the meaningful 'short-term' figure for one-shots. Music is judged on I.
- **True peak**: 4x oversampled (polyphase), dBTP. **Clipping**: samples at |x| >= 0.999. **DC**: |mean| as a fraction of full scale.
- **Duration**: *audible ms* runs to the last sample above -60 dBFS and is checked against the cue budget; *file ms* includes the 20 ms of digital silence appended to very short effects (keeps codec noise off the end).
- **Lead silence**: time to the first sample above -50 dBFS (rule for effects: < 5 ms). **End**: peak of the last 2 ms (rule: <= -50 dBFS, so nothing is cut off with a click).
- **Phone loss**: how much loudness disappears through a 2nd-order 300 Hz high-pass, a rough model of a phone speaker. Above 6 LU means the cue relies on bass a phone cannot play.
- **Loop seam** (music): a click is a short burst of high frequencies. The file is looped (end joined to sample 0) and high-passed at 6 kHz (4th order, with circular context so the filter is settled). The seam passes if the peak within 2 ms of the join is no higher than 2x the loop's own 99th percentile of 2 ms block peaks (the join is no rougher than the music itself) **or** lies below -60 dBFS (at least 34 dB under the music's loudness, masked). Also: loudness of the last vs. the first 400 ms within 3 LU, and, for information, the second-difference ratio (|x[n+1] - 2x[n] + x[n-1]| at the join vs. its 99th percentile). Self-test: the same loop cut at 55 % and rejoined at 80 % must fail. The seam is checked on the decoded OGG because the encoder codes the last and first blocks independently; `generate.py` raises the Vorbis quality until it passes.

## Spectrograms

Waveform (top, dotted lines at -1 dBFS) and log-frequency spectrogram (bottom, 75 dB range) in `spectrograms/`. Reviewed visually; notes below.

Reviewed 2026-11-21 by the sound designer (images read one by one; nobody listened).

- **Keypad** (`sfx_keypad_1`): broadband attack under 10 ms, then only the 385-445 Hz body, gone by about 45 ms. No tonal tail. The three variants differ only in plate and body frequency.
- **Delete**: two taps 22 ms apart with a lower (~300 Hz) body, visibly different from a digit tap.
- **Guess sent**: leather snap (broadband, 0-20 ms), 130 Hz + 400 Hz body, relay latch at 78 ms, done by 160 ms.
- **Lamp ladder** (`seq_lamps_3s2b`, `seq_round_2s1b`): strike fundamentals step 880 -> 988 -> 1175 Hz with bell partials at 2.76x and 5.4x; balls at 587 -> 659 Hz with no high partials and a shorter decay. Every lamp starts with a broadband relay click, so each onset stands out even where the tones overlap.
- **Out**: energy at 150-600 Hz, nothing above ~1 kHz after the click, no ring past ~200 ms. Clearly unlike strike and ball.
- **Solved**: broadband bat crack at 0, bells climbing at 35/95/155/215 ms over an organ swell, faded out by 1.13 s so it is quiet when the result sting starts (+1.09 to +1.42 s, depending on digit count).
- **Win**: three pick-up onsets at 0/70/140 ms (the haptic pattern), D major chord to ~1.7 s, then the room tail.
- **Lose**: all energy under ~1.5 kHz; F#4 -> D4, then a B minor chord from 0.52 s. Soft, no bright partials.
- **Draw**: two equal bell onsets at 0/90 ms (the haptic pattern) over an open fifth. **Match found**: onsets at 0 and 120 ms (the haptic pattern), rings to ~1 s.
- **Timer** (`seq_timer`): warning at 10 s (two ticks + E5 659 Hz), silence at 9-6 s, five ticks with a B5 tone, then time-out with a falling A4 -> D4.
- **Music**: the menu loop sits mostly at 100 Hz-2 kHz with sparse brushes and a chord change every 3 s. The match loop is almost entirely under 1 kHz, with only faint organ harmonics in the 0.8-2 kHz lamp band, so the lamps stay readable over it. Seam plots: both channels run continuously through the loop point.

Defects found in this review and fixed: (1) truncation clicks, i.e. broadband vertical lines where bell and mallet notes were cut off mid-ring (`sfx_match_found` at 0.90/1.02 s, `sfx_quick_phrase` at 0.17 s). Fixed at the source: the partial and thump generators now always fade their ends, which also removed the same clicks inside the music loops (kick and marimba tails). (2) The keypad body rang about 60 ms; it was shortened (decay 16 -> 10 ms) for a drier tap.
