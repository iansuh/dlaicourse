# Phase 2 design review, round 2: Android renders after fix round 1

Reviewer: design lead. Date: 2026-11-25. Build: commit `7d73fc4` ("remove-ads purchase (Play Billing), interstitial policy, design follow-ups"), 450 Roborazzi renders in `android/app/screenshots/`.

Sources of truth: `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `tokens.json`, `screens/*.md` (with `ADAPTIVE.md` §1–2 as rewritten after round 1), the 59 regenerated reference shots in `design/shots/` (tabular digits). The earlier report is [`REVIEW_PHASE2.md`](REVIEW_PHASE2.md). The engineer's deviations are in `android/README.md` § "Deviations from the design".

Method, as in round 1: every reference shot paired with its render (reference | render | 3× diff); every new state, `-small-*` (360 × 320) and `-ko-font200` variant opened as an image; positions measured with PIL at density 2 (2 px = 1 dp); a flat-colour audit over all 450 renders against `tokens.json` (alpha tokens and the 64 % scrim composited, 35 % memo columns included). I read the Compose source only to name the cause and the file. App code was not changed. File paths below are relative to `android/app/src/main/kotlin/app/numbaseball/ui/`.

## Verdict

**Not signed off.** Close, and much better than round 1.

The blocker is gone. At 200 % every guess keeps all its digits, numerals are capped at 1.3×, the duel strip has its two-line layout, and the results, lobby and team screens no longer collapse. 27 of the 36 round-1 defects are fixed, and one more is closed as an accepted deviation. Remove ads, the result link, chat system lines, player-card states, the tutorial and the dialogs all match their shots. Every flat colour is a token.

What still blocks sign-off: four 200 % / small-window states lose content (D2-37, D2-38, D2-39, D2-41), plus the remaining round-1 items D2-03 and D2-34. Transfer mode hides its own field (D2-40). Three areas cannot be reviewed yet: de/ru renders (D2-30), the language picker, the 구매 section at 200 % / small, and the active transfer code (D2-42). Two specified features are still missing: the opponent-log sheet and the server-error dialog (D2-43).

Counts: round 1: **27 fixed, 1 closed as an accepted deviation, 8 open** (5 major, 3 minor). New: **16** (7 major, 9 minor). Open in total: **0 blocker, 12 major, 12 minor**.

## Not compared

- **de / ru (9 shots):** the eight locales now ship (`values-de`, `values-ru` … `locales_config.xml`), but no de / ru renders were recorded. See D2-30.
- **Status bar, gesture pill, sample ad creative (`#D9DCD4`, `#3A3F3B`):** test frame / `LocalAdPreview`, ignored. The 50 dp test banner scales its text at 200 %; this is test content only.
- **Lobby backdrop behind the chat sheet** (`chat-*`): the shot uses a placeholder heading, the render the real lobby. It sits under the 64 % scrim and was not compared.

## 4. Checked and accepted (not defects)

- **Colour:** the audit over all 450 renders found no flat non-token colour. The only exceptions are the test ad creative and the `strikeTint` hero gradient; the remaining off-palette pixels are text antialiasing. The purchase rows use no lamp colour; the error line is `strike` (10.64 : 1 on `bg`).
- **Ad rules:** no banner on Settings (구매 included), match, tutorial, lobby, chat or any 360 × 320 window. The result link sits in the top bar in normal windows only and disappears after purchase. The result band is at y = 725 dp with the 24 dp clear space.
- **Settings contact row** is 66 dp vs 68 in the shot: the shot's height comes from the 48 dp copy button plus the CSS row padding. The spec says 64 dp minimum, so the render is acceptable; I will fix the prototype.
- **New screens that match their spec:** card loading ("–" in `textDisabled`), failed (+ "다시 시도"), not available (the context line drops "온라인 대전 기록"); quick-phrase sheet at 200 % (48 dp buttons, one line by 어절); toasts at 200 % wrap; leave / forfeit / delete dialogs with the 64 % scrim; the overflow menu with 기권 in `out`; tutorial steps at 1.0 and 200 %; the team board with my duel running (`arrow_back`); the large memo sheet's cell visuals and cycles.
